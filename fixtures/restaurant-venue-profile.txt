Tavolino Rosso - Restaurant Venue Profile

Tavolino Rosso is a family-run Northern Italian restaurant at
482 Crestline Avenue, Portland, Oregon 97210, on the quiet end of the
Northwest District. Since opening in 2018 we have hosted rehearsal
dinners, corporate offsites, and milestone birthdays, and we are now
formalizing our private events program with local planners.

Capacity and layout. The main dining room seats 70 guests for a full
seated dinner service. For standing receptions with passed appetizers
we can welcome a few more, but 70 is the number we commit to whenever
everyone is seated at once.

Food and pricing. The kitchen focuses on Northern Italian cooking:
fresh pasta made each morning, wood-fired mains, and a rotating
antipasti board that follows the seasons. Pricing sits squarely in the
upscale range, with most mains between 30 and 45 dollars.

Drinks. We operate a full bar with a dedicated cocktail program,
alongside an all-Italian wine list and a short menu of zero-proof
options.

Amenities. The venue includes a private dining room that can be closed
off for toasts and speeches, an outdoor patio that is heated and
covered year-round, and a projector and sound system for presentations
and slideshows. The entire space is wheelchair accessible, including
the patio and both restrooms.

Service. Every private event is staffed by a dedicated captain, and we
build a custom prix fixe menu with each host ahead of the event.

Event experience. We have run private events steadily for the past
four years - weddings, product launches, and quarterly board dinners.
On a five-point scale we would rate our private event experience a 4:
seasoned, with room to grow into larger productions.

Availability. We are taking private event reservations beginning
March 14, 2026. Weeknights book faster than weekends through spring.

We look forward to hosting your next event.
