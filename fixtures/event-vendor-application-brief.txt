Vendor Network Intake Form - Full Brief

We are Party Place, an events company in Portland, Oregon. We are opening
applications for our preferred vendor network (caterers, photographers,
DJs, florists, stylists, and transportation partners), and we need a
thorough intake form. Please build it from this brief. Everything below
should become a question unless marked as background.

Section 1 - Business identity
- Business name. Required.
- What category of vendor are they? One choice from: catering,
  photography, music and DJ, florals, venue styling, transportation, or
  other. Required.
- How many years they have been in business. Required.
- Full business address. Required.

Section 2 - Contact details
- Name of the main contact person. Required.
- Contact email. Required.
- Contact phone number. Required.
- Website or portfolio link. Optional - not everyone has one.

Section 3 - Services and logistics
- Which services do they offer? They should be able to tick all that
  apply: on-site staff, delivery and setup, teardown and cleanup, custom
  design consultations, equipment rentals, and day-of coordination.
  Required, at least one.
- What area do they serve? One of: Portland metro, Willamette Valley,
  Oregon statewide, Pacific Northwest, or nationwide. Required.
- What size of event do they typically handle? Buckets: under 25 guests,
  25-75, 75-150, 150-300, or 300+. Required.

Section 4 - Business practices
- Insurance status: fully insured, liability only, or not insured.
  Required - this is a compliance gate for us.
- Preferred payment structure: deposit plus balance, full prepayment,
  installments, or net-30 invoicing. Required.
- For caterers specifically we care about dietary accommodations, so add
  a tick-all-that-apply list: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, kosher,
  halal, nut-free. Optional, since not every vendor serves food.

Section 5 - Experience and availability
- Have them self-rate their event experience on a 1-to-10 scale, where 1
  means brand new and 10 means seasoned veteran. Required.
- The earliest date they could take a booking from us, as a real date.
  Required.
- A short written summary of their cancellation policy. Required - our
  planners read these word for word.

Section 6 - The extras
- Any awards or press mentions they want to share. Optional long answer.
- How they heard about the vendor network: referral, social media, trade
  show, search, or other. Optional.
- A final open "anything else we should know?" long-answer box. Optional.

Background (do not turn into questions): applications are reviewed every
Friday, and approved vendors get a listing in our planner portal. Keep
the tone of the form friendly but professional, and keep required
markers accurate - our coordinators filter on them.
